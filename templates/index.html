DETECTION PIPELINE

What happens when you hit analyze

01

Normalize

Text is lowercased, links and emails are tokenized, and noise is stripped so the model reads meaning, not formatting.

02

Vectorize

A TF-IDF vectorizer converts the cleaned text into weighted word and phrase features across a 5,000-term vocabulary.

03

Classify

A logistic regression model — trained separately for job scams and phishing — scores the vector and returns a probability.

04

Explain

The highest-weighted terms behind the verdict are surfaced, so the score isn't a black box.
